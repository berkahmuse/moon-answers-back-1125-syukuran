Entry for [The Moon Finally Answers Back](https://make.fun/c/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes one original single-line comeback, in the Moon's own voice, to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises — and nobody has ever once wished me good night back.

110 characters, one line. It answers all three things the Moon is owed an answer for — the wishes, the howls, the 3 a.m. promises — in a single breath, then turns the whole sky around: after four billion years of listening, the Moon's complaint is that nobody ever wished *it* good night back. The joke lands on the last three words, wry and old and a little tired, exactly how a Moon that has heard everything would say it. Nothing is explained; the reversal is the punchline.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
